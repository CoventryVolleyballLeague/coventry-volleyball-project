Welcome

What started in 2024 as a handful of neighbors picking up a volleyball for fun has turned into one of the best parts of our week. None of us were pros — just a group from the community who fell in love with the game — and that evening get-together quickly became a tradition everyone looks forward to. For us, volleyball time is community time: a chance to unwind, compete a little, and catch up with familiar faces after a long day. This site is where we'll keep everyone in the loop — league updates, live scores, team rosters, photos from game nights, and everything in between. Come for the volleyball, stay for the community. Join us and enjoy!

Standings Abbreviations

Code Meaning How to read it
SWSets WonTotal sets the team won across completed round-robin matches
SLSets LostTotal sets the team lost across completed round-robin matches
SRSet RatioSW ÷ SL (an undefeated team with SL = 0 ranks above any finite ratio)
PFPoints ForTotal rally points scored by the team
PAPoints AgainstTotal rally points conceded by the team
PRPoint RatioPF ÷ PA
MPMatch Points2–0 win = 3 MP (0 MP for the loser); 2–1 win = 2 MP (1 MP for the loser)
Tiebreakers—MP → SR → PR → Head-to-Head → Coin Toss, used for seeding
Playoff Rules—1st seed direct to Final; 2nd vs 3rd Semi-Final; 4th eliminated
